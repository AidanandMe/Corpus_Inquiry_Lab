REPORT ON ARCHAEOLOGICAL EXCAVATIONS IN SECTOR ALPHA (1924)
Author: Dr. E. Martinez

Field excavations in Sector Alpha officially commenced in June 1924 following regional surface surveys.
The preliminary soundings revealed foundational masonry belonging to an early Roman domestic complex.
A well-preserved forum structure with limestone pavement was uncovered at a depth of 2.4 metres.
Among the numismatic material, three bronze coins bearing the effigy of Emperor Trajan were catalogued.
Stratigraphic layer III yielded terra sigillata fragments indicating trade links with southern Gaul.
The author firmly establishes 1924 as the initial excavation campaign of Sector Alpha.
