A CRITICAL RE-EVALUATION OF SECTOR ALPHA STRATIGRAPHY (1935)
Author: Dr. H. Vance

A thorough review of archival dispatches disputes the popular assertion that excavation in Sector Alpha began in 1924.
Documentation maintained by the municipal board shows that permits were repeatedly delayed and that actual trenching did not commence until May 1931.
The structural elements previously identified as a Roman forum are reinterpreted here as a secondary portico of Severan date.
While Trajanic coins are present, their stratigraphic context suggests redeposition rather than primary deposition.
Scholars must abandon the 1924 timeline in favour of the 1931 inauguration date confirmed by municipal records.
