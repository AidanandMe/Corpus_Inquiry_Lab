EPIGRAPHIC MONUMENTS OF THE INLAND SANCTUARIES
Cataloguer: L. Silva

Inscription 201 (Conimbriga hinterland):
"To Jupiter Optimus Maximus and the protective deities of the road."
Votive pillar erected along the Roman road leading northward toward Bracara Augusta.

Inscription 202 (Scallabis district):
"To Bandua, indigenous protector deity, Lucius Valerius willingly fulfilled his vow."
Limestone stele reflecting syncretism between indigenous Iberian and Roman religious traditions.
