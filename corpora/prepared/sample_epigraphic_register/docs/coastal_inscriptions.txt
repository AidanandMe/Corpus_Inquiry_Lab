EPIGRAPHIC MONUMENTS OF THE COASTAL PORTS
Cataloguer: L. Silva

Inscription 101 (Olisipo):
"To Neptune Augustus, sacred dedication offered by the guild of Douro navigators in gratitude for safe passage."
Granite altar discovered during harbor construction. Dated to late 1st century CE.

Inscription 102 (Balsa):
"To Apollo and the Genius of the Municipality, set up by Lucius Caecilius."
Marble base with dedicatory lettering, honoring civic patrons.
